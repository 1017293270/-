Scroll controlled motion system

Make the wheel feel like a timeline.

A standalone homepage motion prototype: wheel-driven chapters, staged text reveals, and parallax geometry that reacts to scroll progress.

Scroll
01 Origin

Foundation

We start with a fixed stage.

The page still uses native scrolling, but each chapter gets extra height. The content stays sticky in the viewport, turning wheel distance into timeline time.

02 Signal

Progress

The scrollbar becomes a director.

Each chapter enters, holds, and exits from the same scroll progress value. Text, lines, background, and navigation all stay locked to one timeline.

03 Method

Parallax

Depth comes from different speeds.

Background geometry moves slowly, foreground copy moves faster, and particles drift lightly. Different speeds create depth without requiring full WebGL first.

04 Future

Next step

Upgrade the stage when the story needs it.

To push it further, replace the canvas with a Three.js scene and bind camera, material, and particle parameters to the same scroll progress.
